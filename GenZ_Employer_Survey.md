# Gen Z Career Aspirations & Employer Gap Survey

Two short surveys, each with multiple-choice questions (4 options each). Designed to be quick to complete, easy to understand, and directly tied to the variables that shape Gen Z career choices and employer hiring/retention challenges.

---

## SURVEY A: For Gen Z Respondents (Ages 18–28)

**1. What is your age range?**
A) 18–21
B) 22–24
C) 25–28
D) Prefer not to say

**2. What is your current education level?**
A) High school diploma
B) Trade/vocational certificate
C) College degree (associate or bachelor's)
D) No formal degree

**3. If you studied a specific field, which category best fits it?**
A) Technology / Engineering
B) Business / Finance
C) Arts / Creative / Design
D) I did not pursue a specific field of study

**4. Where do you currently live?**
A) Large city / urban area
B) Suburban area
C) Small town / rural area
D) I relocate frequently

**5. What is your current job status?**
A) Full-time employed
B) Part-time employed or freelancing
C) Unemployed and job-searching
D) Full-time student

**6. Which industry are you most interested in working in?**
A) Technology
B) Healthcare
C) Creative arts / Media
D) Business / Finance

**7. What best describes the type of role you're aiming for?**
A) Technical/specialist role (e.g., developer, analyst)
B) Managerial/leadership role
C) Creative role (e.g., design, content)
D) Not sure yet

**8. What is the minimum salary you'd accept for your ideal job?**
A) Under $35,000/year
B) $35,000–$50,000/year
C) $50,000–$70,000/year
D) Over $70,000/year

**9. What work arrangement do you prefer?**
A) Fully remote
B) Hybrid (mix of remote and in-office)
C) Fully in-office
D) No strong preference

**10. How important is work-life balance to you when choosing a job?**
A) Extremely important — a top priority
B) Somewhat important
C) Not a major factor
D) Unsure

**11. What matters most to you in a company's culture?**
A) Diversity and inclusion
B) Fun, social, and collaborative environment
C) Professionalism and structure
D) I don't have a strong preference

**12. How quickly do you expect career growth or promotion?**
A) Within the first year
B) Within 1–2 years
C) Within 3–5 years
D) I'm not focused on speed of growth

**13. How concerned are you about job security in your chosen field?**
A) Very concerned
B) Somewhat concerned
C) Not very concerned
D) Not concerned at all

**14. How important is it that your job aligns with a personal cause or value (e.g., sustainability, social impact)?**
A) Essential — I won't work somewhere that conflicts with my values
B) Important, but not a dealbreaker
C) Nice to have, not a priority
D) Not important to me

**15. How much do workplace mental health resources influence your job choice?**
A) A major factor
B) A minor factor
C) Not a factor
D) I haven't thought about it

**16. How do you feel about jobs involving new technology (AI, VR, automation, etc.)?**
A) Excited — I actively seek these roles
B) Open to it, but not a priority
C) Indifferent
D) Concerned about being replaced by it

**17. Do you currently have, or want, a side hustle alongside your main job?**
A) Yes, I already have one
B) Yes, I'd like to start one
C) No interest in a side hustle
D) Unsure

**18. What is the biggest barrier holding you back in your job search?**
A) Lack of experience
B) Limited job opportunities in my area
C) Salary/benefits not meeting expectations
D) Discrimination or bias in hiring

**19. Who or what influences your career decisions the most?**
A) Family/parents
B) Friends or peers
C) Social media or online content
D) My own personal interests, independent of outside influence

**20. Would you relocate for the right job opportunity?**
A) Yes, anywhere (including other countries)
B) Yes, but only within my own country
C) Only for a nearby city
D) No, I prefer to stay where I am

**21. Where do you see yourself in 5–10 years?**
A) In a senior/leadership position
B) Running my own business
C) Still growing in my current field/specialty
D) Not sure yet

## SURVEY B: For Employers/Hiring Managers

**1. What industry does your company operate in?**
A) Technology
B) Retail / Consumer goods
C) Healthcare
D) Manufacturing / Other

**2. What is the size of your company?**
A) Small startup (1–50 employees)
B) Mid-size company (51–500 employees)
C) Large corporation (500+ employees)
D) Not sure / varies by department

**3. Where is your company primarily based?**
A) Large city / urban area
B) Suburban area
C) Small town / rural area
D) Fully remote, no fixed location

**4. What is your biggest challenge when hiring Gen Z candidates?**
A) Salary expectations don't match what we can offer
B) Candidates lack relevant experience or skills
C) Difficulty finding candidates who apply/respond
D) Candidates decline offers for other opportunities

**5. What is the most common reason Gen Z employees leave your company?**
A) Better offer elsewhere
B) Poor fit with company culture
C) Lack of growth or advancement opportunities
D) Burnout or work-life balance issues

**6. How much does your company invest in training new hires?**
A) Extensive, structured onboarding and training program
B) Moderate — some training, mostly on-the-job learning
C) Minimal — new hires are expected to learn quickly on their own
D) No formal training process

**7. What work arrangement does your company primarily offer?**
A) Fully remote
B) Hybrid
C) Fully in-office
D) Varies by role/department

**8. What is the typical starting salary range you offer entry-level Gen Z hires?**
A) Under $35,000/year
B) $35,000–$50,000/year
C) $50,000–$70,000/year
D) Over $70,000/year

**9. Which benefits does your company currently offer? (Select the closest match)**
A) Health insurance + mental health support + generous vacation
B) Health insurance and standard vacation only
C) Basic legally-required benefits only
D) Benefits vary significantly by role/level

**10. How would you describe your company culture?**
A) Intentionally built and clearly communicated
B) Fairly informal, developed organically over time
C) Traditional/corporate and structured
D) Still figuring it out

**11. How do you typically learn what Gen Z employees think about working at your company?**
A) Formal exit interviews
B) Informal check-ins or manager feedback
C) Employee review sites (Glassdoor, etc.)
D) We don't currently track this

**12. What is your primary method for recruiting Gen Z talent?**
A) Job boards (LinkedIn, Indeed, etc.)
B) Social media outreach
C) Campus/university recruiting
D) Employee referrals

**13. How would you generally describe Gen Z employees at your company?**
A) Innovative and tech-savvy
B) Entitled or difficult to manage
C) Hardworking but need more guidance
D) Mixed — depends on the individual

**14. How important is offering flexible/remote work to attracting Gen Z candidates, in your experience?**
A) Extremely important — we lose candidates without it
B) Somewhat important
C) Not a major factor in our hiring
D) Unsure

**15. How does your company address employee mental health and well-being?**
A) Dedicated programs/resources (EAPs, counseling, etc.)
B) Informal support through management
C) No specific program currently
D) Considering adding one

**16. How fast do Gen Z employees at your company typically expect promotion or advancement?**
A) Within the first year
B) Within 1–2 years
C) Within 3–5 years
D) Expectations vary widely

**17. Does your company have a clearly stated mission or set of values (e.g., sustainability, social impact)?**
A) Yes, and we actively promote it in hiring
B) Yes, but we don't emphasize it much
C) No formal mission/values statement
D) Unsure

**18. How does your company use technology (AI, automation, etc.) in day-to-day roles?**
A) Heavily integrated across most roles
B) Used in some departments/roles
C) Minimal use currently
D) Not applicable to our industry

**19. How does your company view employees having side hustles or freelance work outside their job?**
A) Fully supportive, as long as it doesn't conflict with work
B) Neutral — not something we address
C) Discouraged
D) Prohibited by policy/contract

**20. What would most help your company better attract and retain Gen Z talent?**
A) More competitive salaries/benefits
B) Clearer growth and advancement paths
C) More workplace flexibility
D) Better alignment with their values/purpose
