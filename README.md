Project Summary

Gen Z Career Aspirations & Workplace Expectations was an end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding what Gen Z expects from their careers and where gaps exist between employee expectations and employer needs.

I collected and cleaned survey data, performed Excel Pivot Table and SQL analysis, identified key business insights, and created Excel and Power BI dashboards focused on manager preferences, mission-driven aspirations, work environment, career growth, salary, and workplace expectations. The final dashboards provide actionable insights for Recruitment and HR teams to better attract, manage, and retain Gen Z talent.

Skills Used in the Project
